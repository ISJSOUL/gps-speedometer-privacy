GPS Speedometer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

What GPS Speedometer does with your data

Location (GPS speed). GPS Speedometer reads your device's GPS location to calculate and display your current speed, altitude, and distance travelled.

  • Your location is never sent to us — we operate no servers and collect nothing about you.
  • Your raw location is not stored anywhere off your device.

Trip history (stored on your device). If you tap "Start" to record a trip, the app saves that trip's summary — duration, distance, minimum/maximum/average speed, minimum/maximum altitude, the weather at the time, and a human-readable place name for where the trip started and ended (for example "Main St, Springfield"). This is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, is never uploaded, and is deleted when you clear trip history or uninstall the app.

Background location while recording a trip. While a trip is recording, the app keeps reading your location even if you switch away from it, so the trip isn't lost. Android requires a permanent notification to be shown for the entire time this is happening, and recording stops as soon as you press Stop.

Third-party lookups. To show weather and posted speed limits, the app sends your approximate coordinates to these services. Neither receives any account, device, or advertising identifier — only a latitude/longitude:

  • Open-Meteo (weather) — https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API (posted speed limits) — https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

Place names for trip history are produced by Android's own on-device geocoding service.

You can revoke location permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → GPS Speedometer → Permissions; the speedometer simply won't be able to show a live reading without it.

Advertising

Free-tier GPS Speedometer shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising network. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and use them for ad personalization, subject to your consent choices. Where required by law (EEA, UK, California), GPS Speedometer shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time via that form.

See Google's own policies for what AdMob collects:

  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Purchasing GPS Speedometer Premium removes all ads permanently.

In-app purchase

The one-time Premium unlock is billed and processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details — Google handles the transaction and gives us only a purchase confirmation token, used solely to unlock premium features on your device/account.

Data we do not collect

GPS Speedometer does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or files. We ourselves operate no servers and receive no data about you.

Note that if you consent to personalised ads, Google AdMob may use your advertising ID for cross-app ad personalisation, as described under "Advertising" above — you can withdraw that consent at any time via the consent form, or reset/delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

If you use the in-app "Send feedback" option, your email app opens with a pre-filled message that includes your app version, Android version, and device model, so we can reproduce the issue. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your own email app.

Children

GPS Speedometer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
